# Origin

> At first, humans did not know anything. By looking, writing and growing, they came to understand.

Rune begins in the void — before any code, before any name. Everything you meet in this lore is named for the role it plays in that world, not with a generic word. Learn the name once, and the code reads like a story.

## What Rune is
Rune is a **door, not a finished framework**. It is a thin layer beneath PHP that gives you two things:

- a **paradigm** — a way to see code as runes, each made of the same five parts;
- the **ten origin runes** — the first runes, shipped in the Composer package `devneet/rune`.

With them you build your own tools, apps, and even frameworks of your own. Think of a pocket knife: one handle, many blades, and a blade only opens when you need it. A rune you never call is never loaded.

> *"Rune is a lore-driven, modular concept beneath PHP, designed not to dictate development — but to respond to it."*

## Two words from the void
| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Rune** | something we don't know yet |
| **Ethereal** | a place or thing we don't know yet |

Every rune lives in the namespace `Rune\…`, and the core class that opens the doors is `Rune\Ethereal`. You will call it once in this Act, to awaken your first runite.

## Where it is heading
- **Act 0 · The Void** — you read, and run very little.
- **Act 1 · First Resonance** — you build a CLI app and it learns to remember.
- **Act 2 · Understanding Its Flow** — you see how the runes move together, and bundle.
- **Act 3 · Liberation of Forte** — you make runes and apps of your own.
- **Act 4 · The Beyond** — you keep Rune alive without asking its first creator.

Rune is also built and released with Rune itself: its own tools are runites made from its own runes. You will see that loop again at the end of the journey.
