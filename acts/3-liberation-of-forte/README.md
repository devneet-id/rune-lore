# Act 3 · Liberation of Forte

> Freedom in ability. You no longer only use runes — you make them, and you make apps of your own.

**Runes in this Act:** Specter, Minister — the last origin runes — then **runes of your own** in `@liberations/`
**You learn:** to go beyond the flow, to create and engrave a new rune, to write its Phantasm, and to build new runites (apps) from nothing.

Until now Rune gave you its runes. In this Act you give Rune yours. Through the whole Act we build one rune, **Ledger** — a small book of income and expense — and an app that uses it, carries it, and brings it back somewhere else.
